Detection settings

Lower confidence includes more uncertain results. Lower overlap removes more duplicate boxes. Larger models and resolutions cost more CPU time; they do not guarantee a better result. The pretrained models recognize 80 common object classes. The COCO8 option is a tiny training demonstration, not an improved general-purpose model.

Video settings
